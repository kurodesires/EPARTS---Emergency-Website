Emergency Tips

Fire

Raise the alarm, leave belongings, use the nearest safe exit, and gather at the assembly point.

Earthquake

Drop, cover, and hold on. After shaking stops, evacuate carefully and avoid damaged areas.

First aid

Alert the clinic nurse or ERT. Do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger.

Evacuation

Follow posted routes and staff instructions. Keep access roads clear for responders.
